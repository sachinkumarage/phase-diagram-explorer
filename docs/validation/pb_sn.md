# Pb-Sn: assessed system vs NIST and pycalphad

Generated by `python -m phase_diagram_explorer.validation --systems` with phase-diagram-explorer 1.0.0 and pycalphad 0.11.2. The tests in `tests/test_pb_sn_assessed.py` and `tests/validation/` check these values.

**Data.** The Pb-Sn subsystem of the NIST solder database, downloaded from NIST (`scripts/fetch_tdb.py`, verified by SHA256) and not redistributed here:

- Kattner, U.R. (2017), Phase Diagrams & Computational Thermodynamics - Solder Systems, NIST Standard Reference Database 139, National Institute of Standards and Technology, https://doi.org/10.18434/T4759N (Accessed 12 October 2026).
- H.J. Fecht, M.-X. Zhang, Y.A. Chang, J.H. Perepezko, Metall. Trans. A 20A (1989) 795-803.
- A.T. Dinsdale, CALPHAD 15 (1991) 317-425.
- NIST Pb-Sn page: https://www.metallurgy.nist.gov/phase/solder/pbsn.html

Gas constant here: 8.31451 J/(mol K); pycalphad uses 8.3145.

## Eutectic L -> FCC_A1 + BCT_A5

NIST's calculated values (https://www.metallurgy.nist.gov/phase/solder/pbsn.html) are 182.2 °C and 62.13, 18.91 and 96.59 mass % Sn. They are converted with x(Sn) = (w/118.69) / (w/118.69 + (100 - w)/207.2), the database's molar masses, and 182.2 °C = 455.35 K.

| Quantity | This engine | NIST (same database) | Difference | pycalphad |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eutectic T (K) | 455.391 | 455.350 | +0.041 | 455.392 |
| FCC_A1 x(Sn) | 0.28929 (18.91 wt%) | 0.28932 | -0.00003 | 0.28926 |
| Liquid x(Sn) | 0.74119 (62.13 wt%) | 0.74120 | -0.00001 | - |
| BCT_A5 x(Sn) | 0.98021 (96.59 wt%) | 0.98018 | +0.00003 | 0.98021 |

Test tolerances: against NIST ±0.2 K and ±0.0005 in mole fraction. The pycalphad temperature is where LIQUID appears on heating at x(Sn) = 0.5 (bisected to 0.001 K); its solid compositions are 0.01 K below that.

No experimental column: the commonly quoted Pb-Sn eutectic values (183 °C, 61.9 wt% Sn) could not be confirmed against a published assessment, so the eutectic is compared only with NIST's calculation from the same database and with pycalphad. `tests/test_pb_sn_assessed.py` still checks the eutectic against those values with wide tolerances (±2 °C, ±1.5 wt%), as a plausibility check only.
